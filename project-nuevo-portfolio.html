What I worked on

Integration & debugging

I focused on connecting the software pieces and tracking down failures that showed up when the full system was running. That included issues around communication, compute load, sensing, and embedded reliability.

  • Integrated navigation and perception software with the embedded control stack.
  • Helped troubleshoot system-level failures during full robot testing.
  • Worked with the broader team to keep the software structure organized and reproducible.
What the robot did

Autonomous operation

The robot combined LiDAR-based navigation, camera-based perception, motor control, and a robotic arm to complete the course and delivery task.

  • Autonomous mobile navigation and obstacle avoidance.
  • Vision-based target identification.
  • Payload pickup and delivery with a multi-degree-of-freedom arm.
  • Chassis validated through a 6-inch drop test.
