Goal

Build an autonomous mobile robot capable of navigating a maze-like environment, identifying a target, and using a robotic arm to pick up and manipulate an object. The project required integrating mechanical hardware, mobile navigation, perception, controls, and manipulation into one system.

My Contribution

Developed the control architecture coordinating the mobile drivetrain, robotic arm, motor controllers, LiDAR perception, trajectory generation, and object pickup. Implemented OpenCV-based computer vision on a Raspberry Pi and integrated the vision output with the navigation and manipulation logic so the robot could position itself and execute the pickup.

Also worked on the mechanical side of the system, including chassis design and FEA. The chassis was evaluated against the payload and maze requirements and then validated through a 6-inch drop test.

Result

The robot completed the full maze and autonomous task, earning the highest score in the class and the fastest completion time. The final system demonstrated coordinated operation across perception, navigation, motor control, and manipulation while the chassis passed the required drop-test validation.

Engineering Breakdown
